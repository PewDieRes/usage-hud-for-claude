# Privacy Policy: Usage HUD for Claude

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Usage HUD for Claude ("the extension") is an independent Chrome extension that shows your Claude usage limits. This policy explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

## What the extension accesses

When you're logged in at claude.ai, the extension requests your account's organization list and usage figures from claude.ai, using the login session already in your browser. It never sees or stores your password, and it does not read your conversations or access any other website.

## What it stores

It stores only the latest usage figures (percentages and reset times), the time they were fetched, and the most recent error message, if any. This data stays in Chrome's local extension storage on your device.

## What it shares

Nothing. The extension does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. There are no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no remote servers. The only network requests it makes are to claude.ai.

## Removing your data

Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted in this repository with a new effective date.
